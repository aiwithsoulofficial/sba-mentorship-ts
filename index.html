SECTION 8Scope and Limitations

This is mentorship, not therapy. The Facilitator is a qualified naturopath and brings that lens to the work. However, this mentorship is not a clinical naturopathic treatment program and does not replace medical care. The Student is encouraged to continue working with appropriate healthcare providers alongside this program.

This is mentorship and coaching, not psychological therapy or counselling. The Facilitator will hold space, challenge, and support the Student deeply, but is not a licensed therapist. If the Student requires that level of support, the Facilitator will say so and assist in finding appropriate help.

Results: Outcomes depend on the Student's effort, implementation, openness, and consistency. The Facilitator cannot guarantee specific business results, health outcomes, or life changes. What the Facilitator can guarantee is presence, honesty, and genuine investment in the Student's growth.

SECTION 10Dispute Resolution

When you lead with heart and compassion, disputes are easy to settle. Both parties enter this agreement with the shared intention that any concern will be resolved in a way that leaves everyone satisfied with the deliverables under these terms. If the Student is dissatisfied with any aspect of the program at any stage, the following process applies:

  • Step 1: Direct conversation - The Student raises the concern with the Facilitator in writing (email or Telegram). The Facilitator will respond within 5 business days with a proposed resolution.
  • Step 2: Resolution meeting - If the concern is not resolved through written communication, both parties will schedule a video call within 10 business days to discuss and agree on a path forward.
  • Step 3: Independent mediation - If the matter remains unresolved after direct discussion, both parties agree to engage an independent mediator before pursuing any legal action. Mediation costs will be shared equally. The mediating jurisdiction shall be Queensland, Australia.

Neither party will pursue legal proceedings without first completing Steps 1 and 2 above. This clause does not prevent either party from seeking urgent injunctive relief where necessary.
